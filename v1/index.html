Our Roofing Services

Pitched and flat roofs, slate and tile, lead and roofline. Whatever is going on with your roof, these are the jobs we take on across Hampshire, Surrey and West Sussex.

  • Roof Repairs & Maintenance

    Most leaks start small: a slipped or cracked tile, a lifted slate, perished felt or a flashing that has worked loose. We trace where the water is actually getting in, which is not always directly above the damp patch, and put it right with matching materials. Small repairs done before winter stop minor damage turning into rotten battens and stained ceilings.

    Ask about this

  • New Roofs & Roof Replacements

    When a roof is past repairing, with widespread slipped tiles, sagging or failed underlay, a full strip and re-roof is usually the better long-term choice. That means new breathable membrane, new treated battens, and slates or tiles chosen to suit the house. We also build roofs for extensions and new structures, with a written quote setting out exactly what is included.

    Ask about this

  • Slate Roofing

    Slate lasts for decades, but one slipped slate or failed nail lets water straight through. We repair and replace natural and fibre-cement slate, matching size and colour as closely as possible on older roofs. Single slates can be refixed with hooks or tingles, and tired roofs can be stripped and re-slated in full.

    Ask about this

  • Roof Tiling

    Clay and concrete tiles, from traditional plain tiles to larger interlocking profiles, each have their own rules for gauge, pitch and fixing. We replace broken and slipped tiles, re-tile single slopes and carry out complete re-tiles. On older houses we look for reclaimed or close-match tiles so a repair blends in rather than stands out.

    Ask about this

  • Flat Roofing

    Flat roofs on extensions, garages and dormers usually fail at the edges and joints, or where rain sits in dips. We repair and replace flat roofs in EPDM rubber, GRP fibreglass and built-up felt, choosing the system to suit the size, use and access of the roof. Where needed we renew rotten decking and improve the falls so water drains away properly.

    Ask about this

  • Leadwork & Flashing

    Lead seals the joints where a roof meets a wall, chimney, dormer or another slope, and tired flashings are one of the most common causes of leaks. We fit and repair lead flashings, step flashings and soakers, lead valleys and chimney aprons. Each piece is dressed and fixed so it can move with the building without cracking or lifting.

    Ask about this

  • Ridge & Hip Repairs

    Ridge and hip tiles take the worst of the wind, and when the mortar beneath them cracks they work loose and can fall. We rebed ridges and hips in fresh mortar, or replace them with a dry-fix system that is mechanically fixed and needs less upkeep. Loose ridge tiles are worth dealing with quickly, as they leave the top of the roof open to the weather.

    Ask about this

  • Guttering & Roofline

    Blocked or sagging gutters spill water down the walls, leading to damp, stained brickwork and rotten timber. We clear, repair and replace guttering and downpipes in uPVC and cast iron, along with fascias and soffits. New roofline boards are also a good moment to check the bottom edge of the roof, where felt and timber often rot first.

    Ask about this
